Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Relive ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Relive app handles your information.

1. Data Collection

Relive does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your photos, memories, notes, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device.

2. Photo Access

Relive requests access to your photo library solely to allow you to select photos for creating memories. We read photo metadata (date, location) to organize your memories by time and place. This data never leaves your device.

3. Location Data

If you choose to set a "Home Location," this information is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. We use geocoding services to convert coordinates into readable place names. No location data is sent to our servers.

4. Notifications

Relive may send local notifications (e.g., memory reminders, streak alerts, milestone celebrations) if you grant notification permissions. These are scheduled entirely on your device.

5. Analytics & Advertising

The free version of Relive displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. Premium subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience with no third-party tracking.

6. In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are handled through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat may receive anonymized purchase data. Please refer to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from within the app (Profile → Data Management → Delete All Data). This permanently removes all saved memories and preferences from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Relive is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contactus.relive@gmail.com
