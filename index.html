cherish your memories

Relive is packed with powerful features designed to help you organize, explore, and celebrate your photo memories.

📸

Polaroid Memories

Transform your photos into beautiful, flippable polaroid cards. Add personal notes on the back — just tap and flip.

🗺️

Trips & Maps

Automatically organize your geotagged photos into trips. See where your memories happened on interactive maps.

🧹

Smart Cleanup

Swipe through similar photos and keep only the best ones. Free up storage with intelligent duplicate detection.

📊

Analytics & Calendar

Visualize your memory activity with a beautiful heatmap calendar. Track your photo habits across seasons and years.

🏆

Achievements & Badges

Earn fun achievements as you build your memory collection. Complete streaks, reach milestones, and unlock badges.

Privacy First

All your data stays on your device. No cloud uploads, no tracking, no compromises. Your memories belong to you.

✦ How It Works

Three simple steps to
relive your best moments

  • 1
    Select Photos — Pick photos from your library or take new ones. Relive reads location and date metadata automatically.
  • 2
    Create Memories — Your photos become beautiful polaroid cards organized by time and place. Flip them to add personal notes.
  • 3
    Explore & Enjoy — Browse your memories by timeline or trips. Track achievements, clean up duplicates, and revisit past moments.
